# Fuser Song Manager 2.0.0

A complete rewrite of Fuser Custom Song Manager, now with album art, a preview player and a look inspired by the
game's crate screen.

![The library](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/FaberGG/FuserSongManager/main/docs/images/library.png)

## Download

`FuserSongManager-2.0.0-win-x64.zip`. Unzip it anywhere and run `FuserSongManager.exe`. Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit;
nothing else to install.

> The executable is not signed. The first time, Windows SmartScreen may say it protected your PC: choose
> **More info → Run anyway**.

## What's new

**See your songs**
- Your library as a grid of album art, read straight from each song's pak.
- Hover a song to see its genre, year and the instrument of each part, in the game's colours.
- Click a song to open its panel: album art, genre, year, BPM, key, your rating, the custom song's author and your
  notes. Disabled songs are dimmed and songs with problems are flagged with the reason.

**Listen before you play**
- Preview any song in a loop, like the game: four phrases of eight bars, with a bar ruler you can click to jump.
- Beat, bass, loop and lead are four discs with their instrument; click one to mute that part.
- Press `Z` to turn on autoplay: songs start as soon as you select them.

**Manage the library**
- Enable or disable one song or many (Ctrl+click, Shift+click, Ctrl+A). Nothing is moved while Fuser is running.
- Import from `.zip`, `.rar` and `.7z`, including "download all" archives that contain each song's own `.zip` or `.rar`.
  Only complete `.pak` + `.sig` pairs are installed, and anything skipped is explained.
- Search, filter by status, genre and rating, and sort by title, artist, genre, year, BPM or rating, instantly even
  with thousands of songs.
- **Play Fuser** clears the game's custom song cache and starts it with Steam, Epic or your own executable.

**Coming from v1**
- On first run the app finds your v1 library and offers to bring your ratings, notes and authors. The v1 files are
  only read, so v1 keeps working if you go back to it.

## Keyboard

`Z` autoplay · `Space` play/pause · `Enter` enable/disable · arrows move · `Ctrl+A` select all · `Ctrl+F` search ·
`Ctrl+I` import · `F5` rescan · `Esc` clear the selection

## Good to know

- The first start reads every song once to build the library (a few seconds for a few hundred songs). Later starts
  show the list in under a second.
- The preview plays the parts as they are recorded in the pak, mixed together; the game adds its own effects on top.
  Audio is never written to disk.
- Your data lives in `%LocalAppData%\FuserSongManager`; the log files there (Settings → Open log folder) say what
  happened if something goes wrong.

## Known limitations

- Songs from the patched official DLC use the same catalog as v1, but have not been tested with real files yet.
- Windows only.
